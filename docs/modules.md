# System Modules

## Gym Management System Modules
1. **Module 1 - User Management & Authentication:** Handles user registration, JWT login, role management (Admin, Trainer, Member), password handling, and profile maintenance.
2. **Module 2 - Member Management:** Handles registration of gym members, updating details, searching profiles, tracking activity status, and viewing member info.
3. **Module 3 - Trainer Management:** Manages trainer profiles, updates trainer details, assigns members to specific trainers, and views trainer records.
4. **Module 4 - Membership Plan Management:** Handles creation, modification, deletion, and pricing updates of membership plans and tracks plan validity.
5. **Module 5 - Attendance Management:** Logs daily member attendance, provides historical attendance records, daily summaries, and attendance statistics[cite: 1].
6. **Module 6 - Payment Management:** Records membership payment transactions, subscription renewals, tracks outstanding dues, and generates revenue summaries[cite: 1].
